# CRDO — Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd

## One-line Role

Credo is an AI data-center connectivity company focused on high-speed, energy-efficient copper and optical interconnect solutions, including Active Electrical Cables, optical DSPs, ZeroFlap optical transceivers, retimers, and related diagnostics.

## Bottleneck Classification

**Classification:** Controls Scarce Layer / Supplies Scarce Layer

CRDO is not just a generic AI beneficiary. It sits close to the AI cluster interconnect constraint: reliable, low-power, high-bandwidth connectivity between accelerators, racks, switches, and backend network components.

Classification cap:
- Can qualify as A1 Research if revenue growth, customer adoption, and market vote remain intact.
- Execution cannot be upgraded automatically; it still needs setup maturity, entry confirmation, and stop validity.

## Latest Thesis

CRDO’s thesis is that AI cluster scaling increases the value of stable, high-speed connectivity. As GPU clusters expand, interconnect reliability, power consumption, signal integrity, and cluster time-to-stability become increasingly important. Credo’s portfolio addresses this through AECs, optical DSPs, ZeroFlap optical transceivers, retimers, and software diagnostics.

The latest FY2026 results strongly validate demand:
- Q4 FY2026 revenue: $437.0M
- QoQ growth: +7.4%
- YoY growth: +157.0%
- FY2026 revenue: $1.335B
- Q4 non-GAAP gross margin: 68.3%
- Q4 non-GAAP EPS: $1.16
- Ending cash and short-term investments: approximately $1.4B
- FY2027 Q1 revenue guide: $465M–$475M

This confirms CRDO is no longer merely a story stock; it has financial validation.

## Evidence

1. Official IR reported Q4 FY2026 revenue of $437.0M, up 157.0% YoY and 7.4% QoQ.
2. Official IR reported FY2026 revenue of $1.335B, more than tripling year over year.
3. Management said the company helps customers accelerate cluster time-to-stability, maximize GPU utilization, improve network reliability, and reduce infrastructure power and operating costs.
4. Company materials highlight high-speed copper and optical interconnect products up to 1.6T for AI infrastructure.
5. OFC 2026 materials highlighted 400G / 800G ZeroFlap optical transceivers, 800G / 1.6T optical DSPs, and backend-network use cases.

## Anti-Thesis

Main risks:
1. **Margin compression:** The stock reacted negatively after Q4 FY2026 because investors focused on gross margin pressure, even though revenue and EPS beat expectations.
2. **Customer concentration:** If growth is driven by a small number of hyperscaler ramps, any digestion phase or order delay could cause sharp derating.
3. **Price-in risk:** After large revenue acceleration and strong stock performance, a lot of good news may already be priced in.
4. **Optical competition:** Larger networking and optical players may compete aggressively in 800G / 1.6T and related interconnect markets.
5. **AI capex slowdown:** If hyperscalers slow AI cluster deployment, CRDO’s revenue growth could decelerate quickly.
6. **Good news not rising:** If future beats fail to lift the stock, market vote should be considered weaker despite strong fundamentals.

CRDO has been treated as a leader in AI networking and connectivity. However, after Q4 FY2026 results, the stock sold off in extended trading despite strong revenue and EPS. This is not thesis failure, but it is a warning that expectations may be very high.

Market vote should be upgraded only if:

- It holds key moving averages.
- It reclaims post-earnings levels with volume.
